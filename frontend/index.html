Storage & rounds

Deleting a round removes its matches and every snapshot at once; the rows are cleared in the background. A round that was never downloaded as CSV asks for an extra confirmation. Rounds with tracked matches cannot be deleted until tracking stops. The size per round is an estimate, and the database size only drops after "Reclaim disk space".
